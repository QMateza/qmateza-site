# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

South African small business owners — tradespeople, service providers, and micro-enterprises — who are non-technical, primarily mobile-first, and WhatsApp-native. They arrive with a problem ("I'm losing leads", "I need a website") and a healthy scepticism ("is this too complicated or expensive for me?"). They are not browsing; they are deciding whether to trust someone with their business.

## Product Purpose

A WhatsApp-first lead-generation landing page for Qunda Mateza, a one-person web development and AI automation practice based in Johannesburg. The single goal is one action: start a WhatsApp conversation. Success is measured in threads opened, not page views. The site itself is proof of capability — for a non-technical visitor, how it looks and performs IS the portfolio.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, local. The voice of the competent neighbourhood professional: plain words, no jargon, first person singular. Warm enough to feel human, precise enough to feel trustworthy. Three words: **reliable, approachable, local.**

## Anti-references

- Flashy agency portfolios with parallax, video backgrounds, or award-chasing animation — signals "expensive and hard to work with"
- Generic "AI startup" aesthetics (glow effects, circuit imagery, abstract gradients) — contradicts the plain-language, local-pro positioning
- Corporate "we" language, Title Case headlines, or jargon-heavy copy
- Over-designed layouts that obscure the CTA — every element must serve the WhatsApp conversion

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the proof.** Visitors can't evaluate the work directly, so the site itself demonstrates the standard. Clean, fast execution is the portfolio.
2. **Translate technology into outcomes.** Never sell features; sell the job they do for the business owner.
3. **WhatsApp first.** Every page, every section points toward a conversation — not a form, not a quote request.
4. **Local specificity over generic polish.** Rand pricing, South African context, WhatsApp over email. Generic looks imported; local looks trustworthy.
5. **Calm = trustworthy.** Restraint reads as competence. No decoration that doesn't carry meaning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text). Mobile-first — most visitors are on phones on a mobile data connection. Full reduced-motion support.
